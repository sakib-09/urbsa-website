About URBSA

The University of Regina Bangladeshi Students’ Association (URBSA) is a student community for Bangladeshi students studying at the University of Regina.

We keep our culture alive far from home — through cultural nights, celebrations of Eid, Ekushay and Independence Day, and everyday friendships. We also help new students find their footing: housing tips, campus guidance, and a friendly face when they first arrive in Regina.

Culture

Bangladeshi Night, Mother Language Day, Independence Day, and film screenings.

Community

Iftar gatherings, Eid and Chand Raat celebrations, and social nights.

Support

Guidance for newcomers settling into campus life in Regina.
